I am seeking a full-time industry role centered on machine learning and numerical methods. My background is in mechanical engineering, especially computational fluid dynamics and hybrid physics-based, data-driven surrogate models for cardiovascular flows. I am excited about applying these techniques broadly.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in mechanical engineering at **Stanford University** (NSF Graduate Research Fellow), advised by Prof. Alison Marsden and Prof. Eric Darve. My dissertation research develops machine learning approaches to accelerate patient-specific cardiovascular flow simulation using physiologic reduced-order models and electric circuit analogs of the vasculature. I train models in JAX, PyTorch, and TensorFlow; automate geometry-through-simulation pipelines with **SimVascular** and **svMultiPhysics** on HPC; and collaborate on the [**Vascular Model Repository**](https://www.vascularmodel.com/).

Previously I worked with **Pasteur Labs** (Simulation Intelligence intern, Summer 2024) on graph neural network surrogates for fluid dynamics, and at **UC Berkeley**, the **Belgian Nuclear Research Centre**, and **Los Alamos National Laboratory** on corrosion of structural materials in liquid lead–bismuth eutectic.
