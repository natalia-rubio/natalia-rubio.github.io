Last month I successfully defended my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at **Stanford University** (NSF Graduate Research Fellow), advised by Prof. Alison Marsden and Prof. Eric Darve. My dissertation research develops machine learning approaches to accelerate patient-specific cardiovascular flow simulation using physiologic 'electric circuit analog' reduced-order models of the vasculature. I train models in JAX, PyTorch, and TensorFlow; automate geometry -> FEA simulation pipelines with **SimVascular** and **svMultiPhysics** on HPC; and collaborate on the [**Vascular Model Repository**](https://www.vascularmodel.com/).

Previously I worked with **Pasteur Labs** (Simulation Intelligence Intern, Summer 2024) on graph neural network surrogates for fluid dynamics, and at **UC Berkeley**, the **SCK-CEN (Belgian Nuclear Research Centre)**, and **Los Alamos National Laboratory** on experimental studies of corrosion of structural materials in liquid lead–bismuth eutectic.
